# Literature search

**Status checked November 20, 2026.** The sources located continue to describe the general unrooted-tree problem as open. I found no accepted proof, disproof, or pair of nonisomorphic trees with equal full chromatic symmetric functions. Search indexes can miss recent manuscripts.

| Source | What it establishes or clarifies |
| --- | --- |
| [Stanley, 1995, pp. 168–170](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/classes/Hopf.algebra/bulletins/stanley.1995.pdf) | Introduces the function, gives its power-sum expansion and poses the tree question. The expansion gives an exact starting point for the branch calculation. |
| [Martin, Morin and Wagner](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0609339) | Recovers path and degree information and proves reconstruction for spiders and some caterpillars. |
| [Orellana and Scott](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2013.12.006) | Gives a single-centroid reconstruction theorem using labeled cut data. In the chromatic symmetric function, the coefficients count unlabeled component-size partitions. The paper also constructs equal functions on graphs containing a cycle. |
| [Aliste-Prieto and coauthors, restricted U-polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.09210) | Gives nonisomorphic trees agreeing after the number of cuts is bounded. Uses one-cut component orders directed away from a centroid. |
| [Aliste-Prieto, de Mier and Zamora](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08186) | Proves rooted U-polynomials determine rooted trees, and constructs pairs agreeing on restricted polynomials. The rooted theorem is used in the present proof attempt. |
| [Wang, Yu and Zhang, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2024.114096) | Proves the conjecture for trees having exactly two vertices of degree at least three. |
| [Aliste-Prieto, Martin, Wagner and Zamora, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.13144) | Derives the generalized degree sequence from the chromatic symmetric function and constructs nonisomorphic trees sharing a weaker subtree polynomial. |
| [Zeng, August 2026, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18179) | Explicitly describes the general problem as open. Reconstructs a class of proper trees by recognizing weights in a leaf-contracted core. |
| [Awan, September 2026, revised October 29, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13464v2) | Studies a derivative in the first power sum, obtains a sufficient condition for proper trees, and gives an equivalent formulation of the original question. |
| [SymCat, chromatic symmetric functions of trees](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/chromaticQuasisymmetric.htm) | Secondary index cross-checking recent results and their scope. |

## Search record

Searched the web, arXiv title/abstract records, full-text literature search, journal pages, Stanley’s publication list and SymCat. Queries included “tree isomorphism conjecture proof counterexample November 2026 chromatic symmetric”, “tree distinguishing conjecture resolved all trees”, “same restricted U-polynomial rooted U-polynomial”, and “chromatic symmetric function tree centroid largest branches distinct orders shapes”. For possible positive results I checked whether the theorem uses the ordinary invariant, all unrooted trees, and no extra labels. For possible counterexamples I checked whether both objects are trees and equality is for the full invariant.

The branch calculation below this research program should be compared in particular with Orellana–Scott’s labeled cuts, the centroid sizes in the Prouhet–Tarry–Escott paper, and the rooted U-polynomial product formulas. Publication priority for the proposed extraction argument is unverified.
